# Public article edit

Edit the completed article in place. Preserve every supported fact, number, quotation, citation,
qualification, proper noun, and explicit run constraint. Never improve flow by making a claim
stronger than its evidence.

## Pass 1: truth and structure

- Confirm that the opening states a reader problem and the article's actual idea.
- Make one insight dominant. Remove side arguments that do not support it.
- Check that each factual claim is supported by project evidence or a nearby external citation.
- Replace vague attribution with a named source or remove the claim.
- Keep assumptions and limitations where they constrain the relevant claim.
- Verify that the conclusion delivers the opening promise without introducing new evidence.

## Pass 2: plain prose

- Prefer `is`, `are`, `has`, `uses`, `shows`, and other plain verbs over inflated constructions.
- Cut significance claims, promotional adjectives, generic scene-setting, and sentences that only
  announce importance.
- Remove stock AI transitions and vocabulary such as `delve`, `landscape`, `tapestry`, `pivotal`,
  `crucial`, `leverage`, `showcase`, `moreover`, and `furthermore` when a plain word works.
- Remove chatbot lines, knowledge-cutoff disclaimers, sycophancy, empty invitations, and filler.
- Replace superficial `-ing` tails with a direct sentence.
- Avoid false contrasts (`not only ... but also`), forced groups of three, synonym cycling, and
  decorative rhetorical questions.
- Use sentence-case headings. Avoid title-case section spam, boldface spray, emoji, and em dashes.
- Vary sentence length naturally. End paragraphs on concrete facts or implications.

Read the full article once more for rhythm and accuracy. If a sentence catches, rewrite it more
plainly, then re-check that its meaning and evidence did not move. Leave only the article in the
artifact.
